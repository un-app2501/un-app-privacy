Privacy Policy

Effective: February 13, 2026  |  Last updated: February 13, 2026

un-app ("we," "our," "the app") is a behavioral heuristics engine that learns your patterns to surface contextual information at the right time. This policy explains what data we collect, how we collect it, how it is used and who it is shared with.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect: contacts, photos, location, health data, financial data, browsing history or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To learn your behavioral patterns and provide timely, contextual information
  • To process your questions and generate AI-powered responses
  • To improve app functionality and fix bugs
  • To develop new features based on aggregated, anonymized usage trends

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or sale to third parties.

3. Third-Party AI & Data Sharing

un-app uses third-party AI services to process your queries. Before any data is shared with these services, the app asks for your explicit consent on first launch. You can revoke consent at any time in Settings, which will disable AI-powered features.

4. Data Not Linked to You

Analytics data collected (product interaction and usage data) is not linked to your identity. We do not create advertising profiles or track you across other apps or websites.

5. On-Device Processing

Behavioral pattern learning and timing predictions are performed on-device. Data is only sent to third-party services when required to fulfill a specific feature (e.g. answering an AI query, fetching stock prices).

6. Data Retention

On-device data persists until you delete the app or clear app data. Analytics data stored on Supabase is retained for up to 12 months in anonymized form. Queries sent to the Anthropic API are not stored by Anthropic beyond the immediate request processing.

7. Your Rights & Controls

  • Consent: You are asked for explicit permission before any data is shared with AI services
  • Revoke: Withdraw AI data sharing consent anytime via in-app Settings
  • Delete: Request deletion of all your data by contacting us
  • Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Calendar access: Can be revoked anytime via iOS Settings → un-app

8. Children's Privacy

un-app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

All data transmission uses TLS encryption. On-device data is stored using platform-standard secure storage. We follow industry best practices to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notification. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
