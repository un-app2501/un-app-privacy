un-app Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

🔒 The Short Version

All your data stays on YOUR phone. We never see it. We never collect it. We never sell it.

Our Privacy Philosophy

un-app is built on a simple principle: your personal data belongs to you and should never leave your device. Unlike most apps that collect your data and send it to servers, un-app works entirely on your phone.

What Data Does un-app Store?

un-app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Usage Patterns: When you check stocks, order food, or view your calendar
  • Query History: Your recent searches within the app
  • Connected Services: Which services you've linked (Swiggy, Zomato, Google Calendar)
  • Authentication Tokens: Session tokens to keep you logged into connected services

What Data Do We Collect?

None. We do not collect any personal data. We do not have servers that store your information. We cannot see your usage patterns, your queries, or anything else.

Third-Party Services

When you connect services like Swiggy, Zomato, or Google Calendar:

  • You authenticate directly with those services
  • un-app never sees your login credentials
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device
  • Each service has its own privacy policy that governs your data with them

Data Sharing

We do not share any data because we do not have any data to share. Your information never leaves your phone.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device:

  • Uninstalling un-app deletes all data
  • You can clear app data from your phone's settings at any time

Analytics

We do not use any analytics services. We do not track your usage. We have no way of knowing how you use the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the app and notify users. Our commitment to on-device, privacy-first design will not change.
